Cover image

Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image (10MB max), or generate a quick placeholder. Either way, this appears on each sample card in the library — a sample never ships with a blank card.

"Generate cover" makes a small placeholder graphic from the track's title — good enough to fill the card until you have real artwork.

Stems

Stems are extra audio files (Drums, Melody, Bass, etc.) you can bundle together to send clients. Supported: mp3, wav, m4a, aif, flac — 100MB max each.

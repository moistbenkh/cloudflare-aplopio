FAQ

License, delivery, revisions.

What does the commercial license actually cover?

Every track — AI Express or Custom — ships with a full commercial license. You can use it in paid ad campaigns, social, broadcast and client pitches without attribution and without royalty payments. The license transfers to your client when the ad runs under their brand.

How fast is "24 hours," really?

The clock starts once your brief is in (brand, vibe reference, ad length). AI Express tracks are delivered within 24 hours of that brief being confirmed. Custom by the Producer runs 3–5 days because it's composed from scratch around a call with you.

How do revisions work?

AI Express includes 2 rounds — tell us what to adjust (tempo, mood, instrumentation) and we recut within the same 24-hour rhythm. Custom by the Producer includes unlimited revisions until the direction is locked in.

What files do I actually get?

AI Express: 3 tracks delivered as WAV/MP3 via a Drive link. Custom by the Producer adds full stems and a mastered final file, so your editor can work with the raw material in-house if needed.

Can I hear more before I commit?

Yes — two reference tracks are live on the homepage. If you want something closer to your brand before paying, mention it in the order brief and we'll flag it on the call (Custom tier) or in the first draft (Express tier).
